Clear Pricing

Clear Pricing Before Any Work Starts

Nobody likes surprises on the final bill. So Westminster HVAC prices every Westminster job upfront, after a complete diagnostic that looks at the equipment's age, fuel type, and tonnage, refrigerant levels, coil and heat exchanger condition, and the ductwork feeding each room. You'll know the problem, what it takes to fix, and what it costs before you say yes.

The trouble with HVAC failures is that many of them develop where you can't see them. A pinhole leak in the evaporator coil bleeds off refrigerant, the blower bearings wear down a little at a time, and a weak capacitor holds on until the first hot afternoon, when the compressor won't start. If your bills are climbing, airflow drops, or ice forms on the lines, get it looked at while it's still a simple repair.

How old is your system? When a furnace or AC gets past the 12-year mark, it's worth planning the next one before the current one decides for you. Emergency replacements happen on the worst days, in heat waves and cold snaps, when options are few and everyone is uncomfortable. Our team knows how the Westminster climate affects equipment and will tell you plainly whether yours has years left or is running on borrowed time.

Maintenance Plans

Tune-Ups That Keep Your System Running

The best repair is the one you never need. That's the idea behind our seasonal plans. On each scheduled visit, we clean coils and burners, check the refrigerant charge, test the electrical components, inspect the heat exchanger, flush the condensate drain, and swap in a new filter, all matched to the equipment in your home.

We offer flexible maintenance options for homeowners, with spring cooling tune-ups and fall heating tune-ups for most systems. That's especially important in dusty areas, where dirty condenser coils choke heat rejection and drive up energy use. Each home is assessed individually, and the plan is tailored to your equipment's age, your household's comfort needs, and your local climate.

Consider maintenance as a safeguard for equipment you've already bought. Each tune-up keeps the coils clean, the charge correct, and the filter fresh, and that lowers what you pay to run the system while easing the strain on the compressor and blower. For most families, a plan costs far less than replacing the system ahead of schedule.

Proper Sizing

Comfort Sized to Your Home's Demand

Proper sizing matters more than most homeowners realize. Before recommending new equipment, Westminster HVAC works through the details that determine your home's heating and cooling load: square footage, insulation, window exposure, and duct layout. That gives you a system that runs in long, efficient cycles, controls humidity, and holds every room at the temperature you set.

Homes change over time. A finished basement, an addition, or two new bedrooms can overwhelm the single-stage system that once kept up just fine. Modern two-stage and variable-speed heat pumps hold tighter temperatures and use far less energy. Whether you're comparing a heat pump and a furnace-and-AC pair, or ducted and ductless, we'll explain the real trade-offs in SEER2 ratings, operating cost, electrical and venting needs, and installation work.

Common Problems

The HVAC Repairs Westminster Homes Need Most

Homes in Westminster run into a familiar set of heating and cooling failures, and each one calls for a different fix. Westminster HVAC handles them all: no cooling, air that blows but never gets cold, furnaces that won't ignite or short-cycle, frozen evaporator coils, refrigerant leaks, condensers that hum without starting, thermostats that lose their settings, clogged condensate drains, and mini-splits flashing error codes. Our technicians identify the exact fault and target the repair to the component that actually failed.

Some failures can wait a day, and some can't. A bad capacitor just keeps the compressor from starting. A cracked heat exchanger, on the other hand, can leak combustion gases into your living space, and a backed-up condensate drain can quietly ruin a ceiling before anyone notices. Our technicians carry the parts to swap capacitors, contactors, fan motors, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards, seal ductwork that's leaking air into the attic, and recharge refrigerant once a leak has been repaired.

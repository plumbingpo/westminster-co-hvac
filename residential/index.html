No Surprises

Clear Pricing Before Any Work Starts

Effective HVAC service starts with knowing exactly what you're dealing with. Westminster HVAC offers honest, upfront pricing across Westminster, handling everything from a worn capacitor to a full system replacement. Every job begins with a careful inspection of your equipment, including its age, tonnage, fuel type, refrigerant charge, and duct condition, followed by a plain-English recommendation with the price spelled out before any work starts.

The problem with HVAC failures is that most of them develop out of sight. A pinhole leak in the evaporator coil bleeds off refrigerant, the blower bearings wear down a little at a time, and a weak capacitor lasts right up until the first hot afternoon, when the compressor won't start. When bills climb, airflow drops, or ice forms on the lines, have the system checked before a repair turns into a replacement.

How old is your system? When a furnace or AC gets past the 12-year mark, it's worth thinking about the next one before the current one decides for you. Emergency replacements happen on the worst days, in heat waves and cold snaps, when options are few and everyone is uncomfortable. Our technicians know how the Westminster climate wears on equipment and will tell you plainly whether yours has years left or is running on borrowed time.

Seasonal Tune-Ups

Tune-Ups That Keep Your System Running

Each maintenance visit from Westminster HVAC is more than a filter change. Our technician cleans the coils, verifies the refrigerant charge, tests capacitors and contactors, inspects the burners and heat exchanger, clears the condensate drain, puts in a fresh filter, and measures static pressure across the ducts. Small problems are caught while they're inexpensive to correct, and the equipment keeps running clean and efficient.

We offer flexible maintenance options for homeowners, with spring cooling tune-ups and fall heating tune-ups for most systems. This is especially important in dusty areas, where dirty condenser coils choke heat rejection and drive up energy use. Each home is assessed individually, and our technicians tailor the plan to your equipment's age, your household's comfort demands, and your local climate.

The savings add up quickly. Clean coils move heat more efficiently, a correct refrigerant charge takes strain off the compressor, and a fresh filter spares the blower motor. Next to the cost of replacing a system even one year early, a maintenance plan is a bargain.

Right-Sized Systems

Comfort Sized to Your Home's Demand

An undersized system runs nonstop and never catches up on the hottest days, while an oversized one short-cycles, wastes energy, and leaves the house humid. Westminster HVAC sizes every replacement and new installation to your actual home. That means a proper load calculation that accounts for square footage, insulation, window exposure, and duct layout, rather than simply matching the tonnage of the old unit. For ductless mini-splits, it means matching head capacity to each zone so every room holds its setpoint.

Homes change over time. A finished basement, an addition, or two new bedrooms can outgrow the single-stage system that used to be plenty. Modern two-stage and variable-speed heat pumps hold tighter temperatures and use far less energy. Whether you're comparing a heat pump and a furnace-and-AC pair, or ducted and ductless, we'll lay out the real trade-offs in SEER2 ratings, operating cost, electrical and venting needs, and installation work.

Frequent Failures

The HVAC Repairs Westminster Homes Need Most

The majority of service calls we get in Westminster come down to a handful of familiar problems. The air conditioner blows warm air. The furnace won't ignite or keeps short-cycling. The evaporator coil freezes solid, the condenser hums without starting, or a ductless head flashes an error code. Refrigerant leaks, clogged condensate drains, and thermostats that forget their settings round out the list. Our technicians handle all of them, always targeting the component that actually failed.

Some failures can wait a day, and some can't. A bad capacitor just keeps the compressor from starting. A cracked heat exchanger, on the other hand, can let combustion gases into the house, and a blocked condensate drain can soak through a ceiling before anyone notices. We come prepared to replace capacitors, contactors, fan motors, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards, repair leaky ducts that dump conditioned air into the attic, and recharge refrigerant once a leak has been repaired.
